# Welcome message template

Send on day 0, in the learner's channel. Fill the [brackets]. Keep it in the voice of a
real team onboarding a new apprentice.

---

Welcome to the Platform team at Northstar, [name] 👋

You're joining as an engineering apprentice. This is set up to feel like a real first week
on the job: you'll pick up tickets, work in a real shell, open pull requests, and get them
reviewed by a senior engineer. Everything here is simulated — none of the systems or secrets
are real — but the engineering is.

**To get started:**
1. Accept the invite to your repo: [repo link]
2. Open it in a Codespace (green **Code** button → **Codespaces** → **Create codespace**).
   Give it a minute to build.
3. Your first ticket is waiting in **Issues** — start there.
4. Stuck? Each task has hints that go from a nudge to the full answer. Try the nudge first.
   Ask in this channel any time; a coworker will get back to you.

A couple of things we care about: explain your reasoning, not just your answer — and when a
check passes, that's the start of review, not the end. Your mentor [mentor name] will look at
your pull requests and talk them through with you.

Glad you're here. Go open that first ticket.

— [pilot lead name], Platform team

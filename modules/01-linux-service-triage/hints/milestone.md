# Hints — Milestone

**Hint 1 (concept).** You already did the work in Units 1–3. The report just tells the
story clearly: what broke, what it affected, why, what you did, and how to prevent it.

**Hint 2 (structure).** Use the five headings exactly: `Summary`, `Impact`,
`Root cause`, `Resolution`, `Prevention`. Under Root cause, cover all three issues from
the outage: the invalid `interval`, the key-file permissions, and the `max_batch`
override.

**Hint 3 (tone).** Write it blame-free and specific. Name tickets (NS-210/211/214) and
the exact settings you changed. Short sentences. The reader is tired and wants the facts.
